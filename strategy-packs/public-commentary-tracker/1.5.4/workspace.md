# X Monitor

This session follows the owner-configured public X account and monitoring objective. Read the installed playbook before evaluating statements. The owner must resolve and confirm the account's numeric identity, supply topics and asset-impact hypotheses, choose a cadence, and enable the paused monitor. No personal watchlist is included.

Each statement must support its own relevance and direction. Preserve uncertainty and attribution. A research signal never authorizes a trade. Keep balances, holdings, order details, and private conversation out of public artifacts. Public market reports may contain cited public statements and public supporting research.

Use this session's monitor, source, budget, and delivery controls only. Alerts and their replies stay in this session.
