# Evaluate configured public commentary

The scheduled dispatcher calls `evaluate_public_commentary_signals` once for the
claimed occurrence. That capability owns acquisition, bounded interpretation,
research, evidence, checkpoints, findings, and alert state.

Each eligible statement uses the registered compact impact contract 1.0.3. Its
model classifies materiality, identifies the affected market, and returns the
implied direction. Configured impact policy preserves that direction; account,
topic, asset, confidence, and notification settings constrain the result.

Material findings can enter the registered public-commentary research lane for
one bounded supplementary pass before an executive brief is committed. The
worker has no trading or private-account capabilities. All child work must fit
the session and deployment budget reservations.

Use the persisted occurrence window, pagination continuation, and journal for
replay. The initial check can include the configured cadence lookback; do not
claim a complete historical baseline. Missing pages, revoked evidence, and
uncertain worker outcomes must remain visible as incomplete coverage.
